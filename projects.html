How to read the catalogue

Honest scope beats impressive wording.

Project pages distinguish shipped implementation, documented design and work still in progress. Archived experiments stay visible, but they are labelled honestly.

Technical depth is one click away. Open a case study for the problem, approach and decisions. Open Developer notes for component boundaries, protocols, runtime flow, security assumptions and build details.
