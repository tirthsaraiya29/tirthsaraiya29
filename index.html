The things I keep coming back to

01 / Interests

I like software that has to earn trust. That might mean a native Windows component crossing a privilege boundary, a phone proving its identity to a workstation, or an assistant that checks whether an action actually happened before announcing success.

I work across platforms because the interesting problems rarely stay inside one box. I care about the internals, but I also care about whether the thing is understandable, maintainable and pleasant to use.

A little beyond the terminal

03 / Off-hours

I'm interested in the hardware that runs software just as much as the software itself. I like exploring new tools, seeing how systems behave under pressure, and occasionally disappearing into a game with a world worth getting lost in.

Curiosity is useful outside code, too. It keeps the work from becoming the only thing I think about.

My default mode: pull something apart, understand why it behaves the way it does, then see whether it can be made clearer or more dependable. Sometimes that turns into a repository. Sometimes it just turns into a better question.
